Mathematical Architecture & Formula Framework (V2)

Standardized technical specification of the deterministic mathematical scoring engine used in Jinder. Solves capability mismatch and identical ATS fit scores using continuous asymptotic formulations.

1. Operational Problem & Smooth Function Philosophy

Legacy ATS platforms rely on binary keyword matching or coarse step thresholds. In Version 2, all scoring models are strictly continuous and bounded within $[0.0, 100.0]$.

ASD-STE100 Design Principles: 1. Continuous: No single discrete step decides a score. Equations use exponential, logistic, power, and saturation functions.
2. Deterministic: Identical inputs yield identical outputs in under 5 ms. Ties broken by canonical ID.
3. Zero-PII: Formulas never inspect names, emails, phones, photos, visas, ages, or genders.
Smooth Operator Mathematical Equation Operational Role
Saturation $\text{sat}(x, a) = 1 - e^{-x/a}$ Merit dimensions, learner factor, skill depth
Logistic Upside $L(x; k) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-kx}}$ Wage competitiveness and compensation upside
Exponential Decay $e^{-x/a}, \; e^{-0.55|d|^{1.2}}$ Level parity, seniority fit, distance, recency
Power Credit $(h/n)^{1.3} \text{ for } h < n$ Partial credit for candidate skill under target level
Weighted Jaccard $\frac{\sum \min(u_a, u_b)}{\sum \max(u_a, u_b)}$ Occupational taxonomy and multi-skill overlap

Skill Match (SMF) & Composite Product Fit Model

Evaluates multi-skill mastery against occupational requirements using power credit and asymmetric exponential level penalties. Combines 9 dimensions into the primary product fit score.

1. Mathematical Specification (V2)
$$\text{Skill Credit: } c = \begin{cases} 1 + 0.12\left(1 - e^{-(h-n)/1.2}\right) & \text{if } h \ge n \\ (h/n)^{1.3} & \text{if } h < n \end{cases}$$
$$\text{Skill Score: } S_{\text{direct}} = 100 \min\left(1, \; \frac{\sum u_i c_i}{\sum u_i}\right), \quad u_i = m_i \cdot \rho_i^{0.6} \cdot (0.7 + 0.1 n_i)$$ $$F_{\text{level}} = \begin{cases} e^{-0.55|d|^{1.2}} & \text{if } d < 0 \\ e^{-0.28 d^{1.2}} & \text{if } d \ge 0 \end{cases}, \quad d = r_C - r_J$$ $$\text{SMF} = \min\left(100.0, \; \left(0.25 S_{\text{tree}} + 0.50 S_{\text{direct}} + 0.25 S_{\text{trans}}\right) \cdot \Phi\right)$$ $$\Phi = 0.78 + 0.27\left(0.55 F_{\text{years}} + 0.45 F_{\text{level}}\right)$$ $$\text{Product Fit} = \text{clip}\left(100 \frac{\sum w_i P_i}{\sum w_i} + 7(G - 0.45) + 3A\right)$$

Skill Gap Severity & Job Readiness Model (SGF / JRS)

Isolates skill, certification, level, and experience gaps. Computes months to close with parallel execution concurrency ($T_{\text{total}} = \max T + 0.18\sum T_{\text{others}}$) and job readiness.

1. Mathematical Specification (V2)
$$T_{\text{skill}} = \text{clip}\left(\tau \left(E(n) - E(h)\right) \left(1 - 0.30 \min(1, c_{\text{rel}}/0.45)\right) \lambda, \; 0.25, \; 18.0\right), \quad E(l) = (l/3)^{1.6}$$
$$\text{Learner Factor: } \lambda = 1 - 0.16\left(1 - e^{-Y/8}\right) - 0.012 \min(4, e)$$ $$\text{Other Gaps: } T_{\text{cert}} = \text{prepMonths}(1 - 0.5k)\lambda, \quad T_{\text{exp}} = 14\left(1 - e^{-\Delta Y/2.2}\right), \quad T_{\text{lvl}} = \min(24, 6 \Delta r^{1.1})$$ $$\text{Severity: } \text{sev}_k = w_k \left(16\left(1 - e^{-T_k/2.5}\right) + 3 \ln(1 + T_k)\right), \quad S = \sum \text{sev}_k + 6(1 - O)$$ $$JRS = 100 \cdot e^{-S / 70.0}, \quad GSI = 100.0 - JRS$$ $$\text{Parallel Duration: } T_{\text{total}} = \max_k(T_k) + 0.18 \left(\sum_k T_k - \max_k(T_k)\right)$$
Gap Type Formula / Scale Weight ($w_k$) Parallel Duration Role
Must Skill Gap $\tau \cdot (E(n) - E(h)) \cdot \lambda$ 1.00 Scaled by months-to-learn $\tau$ in taxonomy
Certification Gap $\text{prepMonths} \cdot (1 - 0.5k)\lambda$ 0.85 Professional cert prep; non-statutory blocker
Experience Gap $14(1 - e^{-\Delta Y/2.2})$ 0.70 Sub-linear saturation curve (max 14 mo)
Career Level Gap $\min(24, \; 6 \Delta r^{1.1})$ 0.65 Ladder promotion distance (max 24 mo)
Parallel Closing Rule: Humans acquire competencies concurrently. The primary longest gap governs timeline ($\max T$), while secondary gaps incur an $18\%$ concurrent context overhead ($+0.18 \sum T_{\text{others}}$).

Job-to-Job Proximity & Career Pivot Index (JPI)

Symmetric similarity metric between two job requisitions. Evaluates career pivot viability across taxonomy tree, required skills, salary logarithmic parity, and geographic distance.

1. Mathematical Specification (V2)
$$JPI(J_A, J_B) = \frac{\sum_{k} w_k S_k}{\sum_k w_k} = \frac{0.20 S_{\text{tree}} + 0.34 S_{\text{req}} + 0.12 S_{\text{comp}} + 0.08 S_{\text{sec}} + 0.14 S_{\text{geo}} + 0.12 S_{\text{lvl}}}{1.00}$$
$$\text{Salary Parity: } S_{\text{comp}} = 100 \cdot e^{-|\ln(M_a / M_b)| / 0.50}$$ $$\text{Level Distance: } S_{\text{lvl}} = 100 \cdot e^{-0.45 |r_a - r_b|^{1.2}}$$ $$\text{Geographic Proximity: } S_{\text{geo}} = 100 \cdot P_{\text{mode}} \cdot P_{\text{city}}, \quad P_{\text{city}} = 0.25 + 0.75 e^{-d / 700}$$

Recruiter Talent Search Ranking Engine (TSR / TSS)

Ranks candidate applicant pools for employer vacancies. Evaluates shared profile attributes continuously without reading CV text or private PII fields.

1. Mathematical Specification (V2)
$$\text{TSS} = 0.44 S_{\text{req}} + 0.25 S_{\text{sen}} + 0.18 S_{\text{evid}} + 0.08 S_{\text{reg}} + 0.05 S_{\text{audit}}$$
$$\text{Employer Shortlist Order} = 0.60 \cdot \text{Coverage} + 0.40 \cdot \text{TSS}$$
Requisition Fit ($S_{\text{req}}$, 44%) $100(0.65 K + 0.22 O + 0.13 D) + 3A + 2P$, where $K = \min(1, \bar{c}/1.05)$ mean skill credit, $O$ is ANZSCO occupation closeness, $D$ domain fit, $A$ awards held, $P$ preferred certifications.
Seniority Parity ($S_{\text{sen}}$, 25%) $100(0.55 F_{\text{level}} + 0.45 F_{\text{years}})$. Evaluates calibrated level rank difference and exact verified years. Zero keyword-matching on job titles.
Evidence Rigor ($S_{\text{evid}}$, 18%) $0.50 \cdot 100(1 - e^{-Q/4}) + 0.30 \cdot \text{Cert} + 0.20 \cdot \text{Award}$ with $Q = \sum (l_s/5)^2(0.7 + 0.3\min(1, y_s/4))\kappa_s$. Evaluates verified skill levels and depth; ignores raw CV text length.
Certification Readiness ($S_{\text{reg}}$, 8%) Measures readiness for certifications listed on the job ($100(0.8R + 0.2P')$). Missing certs are never a legal blocker (no rigid statutory hard-locks).
Profile Audit ($S_{\text{audit}}$, 5%) Verifies data contract schema integrity of shared profile attributes (levels, years, skills, education, location).
