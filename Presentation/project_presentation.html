The Bottleneck for International Talent

  • Non-standard Terminology: Overseas job titles ("System Engineer", "R&D Specialist") do not match Australian ANZSCO taxonomy.

The Bottleneck for Australian Employers

  • Unverified Credentials: Difficulty assessing international university syllabi against Australian Qualification Frameworks (AQF).
  • Keyword Matching Failure: Inability to distinguish statutory blockers from tools that can be learned on the job.
  • High Sourcing Cost: Protracted recruitment cycles of 45+ days for mid/senior software engineers.

Mathematical Rigor

Zero Black-Box Scoring.
6 Transparent Formulas (F-01 to F-06).

Instead of unreliable neural guessing, Jinder employs 6 verifiable mathematical formulations with clear weights, level decay, and pool benchmarking.

F-01

Skill Match Frequency (SMF)

Weighted skill coverage separating mandatory skills ($w=2.0$) from preferred skills ($w=1.0$) with proficiency level ratios.

F-02

Gap Severity & Readiness (GSI / JRS)

Penalizes statutory blockers ($\beta=15.0$) while treating learnable tools with gentle decay ($\beta=5.0$) to calculate true job readiness.

F-03

Job Proximity Index (JPI)

Vector distance matrix measuring competency transferability between different Australian ICT career tracks.

F-04

Relative Merit Score (RMS)

Zero-PII pool normalization. Standardizes candidate standing relative to the active applicant cohort using z-score scaling.

F-05

Feed Ranking Score (FRS)

Powers the candidate's personalized feed by fusing SMF (50%), JRS (25%), experience level fit (15%), and behavioral affinity loops.

F-06

Recruiter Talent Search (TSS)

Ranks talent cards for recruiters based on verified core capability match, experience seniority, and profile recency.
