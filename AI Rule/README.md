## Quick Reference: Core Boundaries

1. **Zero-PII**: No names, emails, phone numbers, or photos reach employers or scoring logic.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop**: AI cannot reject or hire candidates autonomously.
3. **Deterministic Math**: Formulas F-01 to F-06 calculate scores without black-box neural bias.
4. **Verified Taxonomy**: All skills match the Australian ICT Taxonomy (172 skills, 53 roles).
5. **Three Bounded Domains**: Software Engineering, AI & Machine Learning, Data.
