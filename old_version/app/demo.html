Jinder Platform Terms & Privacy Notice

1. Scope and Jinder Platform Operation

Jinder ("Tinder for Jobs") delivers algorithmic mutual-fit matching between candidates and Australian vacancies based on the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) ANZSCO occupational standard. Users agree to fair, transparent recruitment conduct.

2. Automated Competency Extraction & AI Badges

Curriculum vitae and vacancy descriptions are analyzed via machine intelligence. Extracted skills are tagged with an "AI Suggested" confirmation badge until verified by the candidate or hiring manager. All users retain perpetual rights to correct or delete AI suggestions.

3. Candidate Anonymity & Zero-PII Protections

Under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) and anti-discrimination laws, candidate personal contact details (legal name, phone, email, street address) are strictly scrubbed from employer views. Candidates are presented exclusively via an anonymized Alias (e.g., SilverKangaroo84) until an interview checkpoint is mutually scheduled.

4. Accuracy and Truthful Representations

Candidates warrant that uploaded CVs reflect genuine work history. Employers warrant that posted vacancies represent active, budgeted roles located in Australia or compliant with remote employment visas.

5. Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) Compliance

Personal data is handled in strict compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Raw resumes are stored in encrypted form and never shared with third-party advertising brokers.

6. Vacancy Discrepancy Reporting

Candidates may report misleading, discriminatory, or expired listings. Any job requisition accumulating 3 or more distinct seeker reports is immediately suspended platform-wide pending administrative review.

7. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia. Jurisdiction rests with the state and federal courts of NSW.
