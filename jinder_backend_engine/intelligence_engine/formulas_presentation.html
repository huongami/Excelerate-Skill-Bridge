Mathematical Architecture & Formula Framework

Standardized technical specification of the deterministic mathematical scoring engine used in Jinder. Solves capability mismatch for international job seekers in Australia.

1. Operational Problem & Technical Solution

Conventional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) use keyword matching. This causes high rejection rates for international applicants because overseas qualifications use different job titles and wording.

ASD-STE100 Core Principle: The Intelligence Engine does not guess. The system applies six exact mathematical models to compare candidate skills, Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) ANZSCO benchmarks, and statutory requirements.
Formula Module Mathematical Objective
F-01 (SMF) Skill Match Function Aligns candidate verified skills with ABS ANZSCO occupational tree.
F-02 (SGF) Skill Gap Severity Isolates statutory blockers (AHPRA, CPA) from learnable software tools.
F-03 (JPI) Job Proximity Index Calculates career pivot distance and compensation parity across jobs.
F-04 (RMS) Candidate Benchmarking Measures relative candidate merit without Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
F-05 (FRS) Seeker Feed Ranking Ranks live Australian vacancies using skills, salary, location, and recency.
F-06 (TSS) Recruiter Talent Search Ranks candidate applicants against employer requisition requirements.
2. End-to-End Mathematical Pipeline
[INPUT STAGE]
• Verified Candidate Profiles & Skill Inventory (Platform Native)
• ABS ANZSCO 2026 Taxonomy (6-digit occupational hierarchy)
• Live Platform Job Requisitions & Direct Employer Vacancies
[MATHEMATICAL ENGINE]
• Continuous logarithmic experience scaling Φ(Y)
• Parallel learning bridge duration max(T) + 0.18ΣT
• Continuous Gaussian seniority parity S_sen
• Learnability factor λ_lrn based on degree level
[OUTPUT STAGE]
• Differentiated decimal scores (e.g. 90.2% vs 88.5%)
• Zero-PII candidate shortlist for employers
• 12-Month Dual Line Projection Curves

Mathematical Audit Commitments

Zero Static Defaults: Every applicant and job has distinct, continuous decimal scores.
Zero PII Leakage: Candidate scores are computed without name, phone, or gender bias.
Deterministic Execution: Identical inputs yield identical outputs in under 5 milliseconds.

Candidate vs ANZSCO Skill Match Model (SMF)

Quantifies how accurately an international candidate's verified skills and experience align with the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) standard occupational definition.

1. Mathematical Specification
$$\text{SMF}(C, O) = \min\left(100.0, \; \left( 0.25 S_{\text{tree}} + 0.50 S_{\text{direct}} + 0.25 S_{\text{trans}} \right) \times \Phi(Y_C, L_O)\right)$$
$$\Phi(Y_C, L_O) = 0.80 + 0.15 \ln\left(1 + \frac{Y_C}{Y_{\text{req}}(L_O)}\right) + 0.05 \min\left(2.0, \; \frac{Y_C}{Y_{\text{req}}(L_O)}\right)$$
$S_{\text{tree}}$ (25%) ABS ANZSCO hierarchical tree distance: 100% exact 6-digit match, 85% 4-digit unit group match, 65% 2-digit sub-major match.
$S_{\text{direct}}$ (50%) IDF-weighted overlap between candidate skills and ANZSCO core competencies: $100 \times \frac{\sum_{s \in \mathcal{M}} \text{IDF}(s)}{\sum_{s \in \mathcal{R}} \text{IDF}(s)}$.
$S_{\text{trans}}$ (25%) Transferable methodology agility score (quality assurance, crisis stabilization, leadership, compliance).
$\Phi(Y_C, L_O)$ Continuous logarithmic experience multiplier calibrated against ANZSCO Skill Level ($L_O$). Clamped to $[0.75, 1.15]$.
ASD-STE100 Procedural Rule: Do not penalize missing optional tools in this formula. Quantify all skill deficits in Formula 2.

Verified Worked Example: SilverKangaroo84 (Registered Nurse)

Input Data: $Y_C = 10.0$ years experience, ANZSCO target = 254411 (Skill Level 1, $Y_{\text{req}} = 5.0$ years).
Step 1 (Taxonomy): 6-digit code matches target ANZSCO exactly: S_tree = 100.0.
Step 2 (Direct Overlap): 8 of 10 core clinical competencies verified: S_direct = 81.7.
Step 3 (Transferable Agility): 5 transferable competencies verified: S_trans = 85.0.
Step 4 (Experience Multiplier): $\text{Ratio} = 10.0 / 5.0 = 2.0$. Multiplier $\Phi = 0.80 + 0.15 \ln(3.0) + 0.05(2.0) = 0.80 + 0.165 + 0.10 = \mathbf{1.065}$.
Step 5 (Composite SMF): Base $= (0.25 \times 100) + (0.50 \times 81.7) + (0.25 \times 85.0) = 25.0 + 40.85 + 21.25 = 87.1$.
Final $\text{SMF} = \min(100.0, \; 87.1 \times 1.065) = \mathbf{92.8\%}$.

Skill Gap Severity & Learnability Model (SGF)

Detects what an applicant lacks relative to a job requisition. Distinguishes hard statutory licensing barriers from learnable software frameworks, and projects concurrent closing time in months.

1. Mathematical Specification
$$GSI(C, J) = \min\left(95.0, \; \max\left(2.0, \; \sum_{k=1}^{m} W_{\text{cat}}(g_k) \times \left(14.0 + 4.5 \ln(1 + T_k \cdot \lambda_{\text{lrn}})\right) + S_{\text{sen\_deficit}}\right)\right)$$
$$JRS(C, J) = \max\left(5.0, \; 100.0 - GSI(C, J)\right) \quad \Big| \quad T_{\text{total}} = \max_k(T_k \cdot \lambda_{\text{lrn}}) + 0.18 \sum_{k \neq \max} (T_k \cdot \lambda_{\text{lrn}})$$
Category Code Classification Name Weight ($W$) Base Duration
CAT-1 Statutory License / Legal Registration (AHPRA, CPA, NER) 1.00 8.0 months
CAT-2 Core Discipline Competency (Clinical Triage, Distributed Systems) 0.60 4.0 months
CAT-3 Tool, Platform, or Framework (Snowflake, dbt, Revit, Epic) 0.25 1.5 months
CAT-4 Local Standard / Orientation (PBS billing, WHS compliance) 0.10 0.8 months
Learnability Acceleration Factor ($\lambda_{\text{lrn}}$): $$\lambda_{\text{lrn}} = \max\left(0.65, \; \min\left(1.05, \; 1.0 - 0.16 \min\left(1.0, \frac{Y_C}{12.0}\right) - 0.05 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{Degree}}\right)\right)$$ Experienced candidates with higher degrees learn faster. This reduces both the duration and gap severity.

Verified Worked Example: Overseas Nurse bridging to Australia

Candidate: 10.0 years clinical experience, Bachelor Degree. Target: Registered Nurse Sydney.
Gaps Identified: (1) AHPRA Registration (CAT-1, 8.0 mo), (2) Australian PBS Billing Guidelines (CAT-4, 0.8 mo).
Learnability Factor: $\lambda_{\text{lrn}} = 1.0 - 0.16(10/12) - 0.05(1) = 1.0 - 0.133 - 0.05 = \mathbf{0.817}$.
Calibrated Durations: $T_1 = 8.0 \times 0.817 = 6.54$ mo. $T_2 = 0.8 \times 0.817 = 0.65$ mo.
Severity Calculation:
$S_1 = 1.00 \times [14.0 + 4.5 \ln(1 + 6.54)] = 14.0 + 4.5(2.02) = 23.09$ pts.
$S_2 = 0.10 \times [14.0 + 4.5 \ln(1 + 0.65)] = 0.10 \times [14.0 + 4.5(0.50)] = 1.63$ pts.
$\text{GSI} = 23.09 + 1.63 = \mathbf{24.7\%}$. $\text{JRS} = 100 - 24.7 = \mathbf{75.3\%}$.
Parallel Bridge Time: $T_{\text{total}} = 6.54 + (0.18 \times 0.65) = 6.54 + 0.12 = \mathbf{6.7\text{ calendar months}}$.

Job-to-Job Proximity & Transition Matrix (JPI)

Measures the distance between two job requisitions. Recommends safe horizontal career pivots and evaluates salary and location parity across employment vacancies.

1. Mathematical Specification
$$\text{JPI}(J_A, J_B) = 0.40 S_{\text{tree}} + 0.35 S_{\text{req\_jac}} + 0.15 S_{\text{sal\_par}} + 0.10 S_{\text{loc\_prox}}$$
$$S_{\text{sal\_par}} = \max\left(0.0, \; 100.0 - 150 \cdot \frac{|\text{Mid}_A - \text{Mid}_B|}{\max(\text{Mid}_A, \text{Mid}_B)}\right)$$
$S_{\text{tree}}$ (40%) Taxonomy tree proximity based on ABS ANZSCO shared unit group (4-digit) or minor group (2-digit).
$S_{\text{req\_jac}}$ (35%) Jaccard similarity between requirement sets: $\frac{|\mathcal{R}_A \cap \mathcal{R}_B|}{|\mathcal{R}_A \cup \mathcal{R}_B|} \times 100$.
$S_{\text{sal\_par}}$ (15%) Salary midpoint equilibrium score. Penalizes compensation gaps exceeding 25% of baseline.
$S_{\text{loc\_prox}}$ (10%) Geographic distance score: Same city = 100.0, same state = 80.0, interstate = 50.0.

Verified Worked Example: Registered Nurse vs Clinical Coordinator

Role A: Registered Nurse (ANZSCO 254411, Mid: $110,000 AUD, Sydney).
Role B: Clinical Care Coordinator (ANZSCO 254413, Mid: $125,000 AUD, Sydney).
Taxonomy ($S_{\text{tree}}$): Share 4-digit unit group 2544 (Registered Nurses) = 85.0.
Skill Jaccard ($S_{\text{req\_jac}}$): Share 6 out of 8 required clinical tasks = 75.0.
Salary Parity: Variance $= (125k - 110k)/125k = 12.0\%$. $S_{\text{sal\_par}} = 100 - (150 \times 0.12) = \mathbf{82.0}$.
Location ($S_{\text{loc\_prox}}$): Both in Sydney, NSW = 100.0.
Composite JPI: $(0.40 \times 85) + (0.35 \times 75) + (0.15 \times 82) + (0.10 \times 100) = 34.0 + 26.25 + 12.3 + 10.0 = \mathbf{82.6\%}$.

Candidate-to-Candidate Benchmarking Model (RMS)

Evaluates multi-candidate applicant cohorts for recruiters using Zero-PII merit parameters. Powers the overlaid radar charts and Head-to-Head hiring decision matrix.

1. Mathematical Specification
$$\text{RMS} = \alpha S_{\text{depth}} + \beta S_{\text{exp}} + \gamma S_{\text{evid}} + \delta S_{\text{trans}} - \epsilon P_{\text{gap}} + \text{EduBonus}$$
$$S_{\text{exp}} = 100 \left(1 - e^{-Y_C / 5.2}\right) \quad \Big| \quad \Delta(C_A, C_B) = \text{RMS}_A - \text{RMS}_B$$
Skill Depth ($S_{\text{depth}}$) $30.0 + 40.0(1 - e^{-N_{\text{dir}}/3.0}) + 30.0(1 - e^{-N_{\text{trans}}/4.0})$. Continuous asymptotic growth.
Experience Maturity ($S_{\text{exp}}$) Smooth exponential model: 2 yrs $\rightarrow$ 32%, 5 yrs $\rightarrow$ 62%, 10 yrs $\rightarrow$ 85%, 15 yrs $\rightarrow$ 94%.
Evidence Rigor ($S_{\text{evid}}$) $40.0 + 35.0(1 - e^{-\text{words}/120}) + 25.0 \min(1.0, \frac{\text{metrics}}{4.0})$. Rewards verified quantitative metrics.
Hiring Decision ($\Delta$) If $|\Delta| > 8.0$: Clear competitive advantage. If $|\Delta| \le 8.0$: Equivalent competency band.

Verified Worked Example: Head-to-Head Comparison

Candidate A (SilverKangaroo84): 10 yrs exp, 8 skills, Bachelor Degree. $\text{RMS} = \mathbf{88.2}$.
Candidate B (AzureKangaroo30): 12 yrs exp, 6 skills, High School Diploma. $\text{RMS} = \mathbf{81.3}$.
Score Delta: $\Delta = 88.2 - 81.3 = \mathbf{+6.9\text{ pts}}$.
ASD-STE100 Verdict: $|\Delta| = 6.9 \le 8.0$ pts. Equivalent competency band. Recruiter must differentiate by specific statutory accreditation and shift availability.

Job Seeker Feed Ranking Engine (JFR / FRS★)

Ranks hundreds of live Australian vacancies for an active candidate. Incorporates behavioural feedback loops (saved employers, saved sectors, ignored penalties).

1. Mathematical Specification
$$\text{FRS} = (0.45 S_{\text{cap}} + 0.20 S_{\text{wage}} + 0.20 S_{\text{loc}} + 0.15 S_{\text{rec}}) \times (1 - \delta_{\text{div}})$$
$$\text{FRS}^\star = \min\left(100.0, \; \text{FRS} \times (1.10)^{\mathbb{I}_{\text{save\_emp}}} \times (1.05)^{\mathbb{I}_{\text{save\_sec}}} \times (0.75)^{\mathbb{I}_{\text{ignore}\ge 3}}\right)$$
$S_{\text{cap}}$ (45%) Candidate capability alignment based on ANZSCO taxonomy match and verified skill overlap.
$S_{\text{wage}}$ (20%) Compensation upside relative to Australian industry sector median.
$S_{\text{loc}}$ (20%) Location match to candidate preference (Sydney: 100%, NSW: 82%, Interstate: 58%).
$S_{\text{rec}}$ (15%) Recency exponential decay: $100 \cdot e^{-0.04 \cdot \text{days\_old}}$.
$\delta_{\text{div}}$ Employer diversity penalty: Prevents single-employer saturation ($\min(0.40, 0.15 \times \text{count})$).

Verified Worked Example: Live Vacancy Ranking

Requisition: Lead Registered Nurse at East Bridge Recruitment ($135,000 AUD, Sydney, Posted 2 days ago).
Scores: $S_{\text{cap}} = 98.0$, $S_{\text{wage}} = 85.0$, $S_{\text{loc}} = 100.0$, $S_{\text{rec}} = 100 e^{-0.04(2)} = 92.3$.
Base FRS: $(0.45 \times 98) + (0.20 \times 85) + (0.20 \times 100) + (0.15 \times 92.3) = 44.1 + 17.0 + 20.0 + 13.8 = \mathbf{94.9\%}$.
Behavioural Boost: Seeker previously saved East Bridge vacancies: $\text{FRS}^\star = 94.9 \times 1.10 = \mathbf{100.0\%}$ (clamped).

Recruiter Talent Search Ranking Engine (TSR / TSS)

Ranks candidate applicant pools for employer vacancies. Eliminates identical scores through multi-factor continuous mathematical curves.

1. Mathematical Specification
$$\text{TSS} = 0.40 S_{\text{req}} + 0.25 S_{\text{sen}} + 0.15 S_{\text{evid}} + 0.15 S_{\text{reg}} + 0.05 S_{\text{audit}}$$
$$S_{\text{sen}} = \begin{cases} \min\left(100.0, \; 92.0 + 3.0 \ln\left(1 + (Y_C - Y_{\text{req}})\right)\right) & \text{if } Y_C \ge Y_{\text{req}} \\ \max\left(25.0, \; 92.0 \cdot e^{-0.15 \frac{(Y_{\text{req}} - Y_C)^2}{Y_{\text{req}}}}\right) & \text{if } Y_C < Y_{\text{req}} \end{cases}$$
Requisition Fit ($S_{\text{req}}$, 40%) $\text{Base}_{\text{anzsco}} + (24.0 \times \text{SkillCoverage}) + \text{EduBonus}$. Differentiates candidates by exact required JD skills.
Seniority Parity ($S_{\text{sen}}$, 25%) Smooth Gaussian decay for experience deficits, logarithmic bonus for experience surplus. No clamped step caps.
Evidence Rigor ($S_{\text{evid}}$, 15%) $35 + 35(1 - e^{-N_{\text{dir}}/2.5}) + 18(1 - e^{-N_{\text{trans}}/3.5}) + 12 \min(1.0, \frac{\text{metrics}}{3})$.
Statutory Readiness ($S_{\text{reg}}$, 15%) Verified Australian licensing (AHPRA, CPA, NER): 96% - 98%; overseas eligibility pending: 82%; unverified overseas: 62%.
Contract Audit ($S_{\text{audit}}$, 5%) Verifies data contract schema integrity across Lakehouse pipelines (100.0%).
